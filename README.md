# CoverCraft

Free cover letter generator: enter the role, company and your key achievements to get a structured, tailored letter in 3 tones — with word-count check.

## Features
- Structured letter builder from role, company and your key achievements
- Three tones — Professional, Friendly, Confident — matched to the job
- One-click copy plus live word-count length check (250–350 word sweet spot)
